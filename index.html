What ReadySpaces is

Think of a big warehouse split into private rooms, each with its own locking door. Every business rents a room and shares the rest: loading docks, forklifts, WiFi, a kitchen and meeting rooms. ReadySpaces says one monthly price covers it all (a deposit and our own insurance are on top).

  1. Step 1

    Rent a private room

    ReadySpaces says its rooms run 200 to 5,000 sf.

  2. Step 2

    Share the big stuff

    Loading docks (doors at truck-bed height), forklifts, WiFi, a kitchen and conference rooms.

  3. Step 3

    Pay one monthly price

    The quote bundles shared building costs, power, internet, cleaning and trash.
